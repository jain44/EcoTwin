What is EcoTwin?

EcoTwin is a web app that helps college students track their daily carbon footprint in a fun, game-like way. Instead of just showing boring numbers, the app gives each student a digital avatar (a "twin") that visually changes based on how eco-friendly their habits are. If you walk to campus and eat vegetarian, your twin looks healthy and green. If you skip habits, it wilts. This emotional feedback loop makes students actually care about their impact.

The app also has a real-time leaderboard where students compete department-by-department, a shared campus forest that grows as more people log habits, and an admin dashboard for faculty to see college-wide sustainability data without manual reporting.

Problem We Are Solving

  • Most sustainability apps are boring — students open them once and forget them.
  • Generic CO₂ numbers don't connect to a college student's daily campus life.
  • There is no accountability or peer comparison, so there's no motivation to improve.
  • Faculty have no easy way to track department-level or campus-level sustainability progress.

How EcoTwin Solves It

  • Gamification — avatar that evolves, badges, coins, department rivalry leaderboard.
  • Physical integration — QR codes at real campus locations verify that students actually visited the canteen, library, etc.
  • AI personality — the twin gives a unique motivational message every day powered by an AI (Groq Llama-3).
  • Faculty dashboard — real-time analytics for teachers, zero manual data entry.

What We Built – Features

1. Digital Eco-Twin Avatar

Each student has an animated SVG avatar that has 3 states — Thriving 🌳, Neutral 🌱, and Wilting 🥀. The state is decided by the student's average carbon emissions over the last 7 days. The avatar also gets a unique AI-generated message every day (plant-themed, encouraging).

2. Daily Habit Logging (Carbon Calculator)

Students log 3 things every day:

  • Commute — how they travelled (walk, bus, train, car, bike)
  • Diet — what they ate (vegan, vegetarian, non-veg, beef)
  • Energy — how long fans/AC/lights were on

The app calculates their CO₂ footprint and compares it to a Mumbai student baseline of 5.5 kg CO₂/day.

3. QR Code Location Proof

There are printable QR code cards for 6 campus locations (Canteen, Library, Train Station, Eco Garden, Hostel, Sports Complex). When a student scans a QR at that location, the habit form is pre-filled and marked as "QR Verified" — they get a bonus Trust Score for that. This ensures logging is real, not fake.

4. EcoBattles – Live Leaderboard

A real-time leaderboard powered by Firebase shows all students ranked by their sustainability score. Students can filter by department or hostel/day-scholar. 15 students from all 8 TCET departments are seeded automatically. This creates healthy rivalry between branches.

5. Predictive Emission Warning

The app runs a simple linear regression on the student's last 7 days of data and predicts where their emissions are headed over the next 3 days. If the trend is rising, a warning banner appears on the dashboard with specific suggestions to fix it.

6. Campus Forest (Shared)

A public page (no login needed) shows a shared forest where every student who logs habits plants a tree. Tree size and colour is based on the student's sustainability score. The forest updates in real-time as people log.

7. Admin / Faculty Dashboard

Password-protected page for faculty at /admin. Shows:

  • Total active students, average CO₂ footprint, estimated CO₂ prevented, app adoption percentage
  • Bar chart of departments colour-coded by performance
  • Top 8 students table
  • All data is live from Firebase — no manual entry needed

8. GreenCoins Marketplace

Students earn coins for every habit logged. Coins can be redeemed for real rewards: canteen discount, free library printing, tree planting certificate, campus bike pass. A streak multiplier rewards consistent daily logging.

9. Achievement Badges

8 badges that unlock automatically: First Step, Week Warrior, Carbon Hero, Commute King, Diet Champ, Power Saver, QR Scout, Eco Legend.

10. Weekly Eco-Challenges 🎯 (New)

Dedicated page at /challenges featuring 4 weekly challenges (No-Meat Week, Zero-Car Week, Low-Energy Week, Log Every Day) with live progress tracking and extra coin rewards.

11. Personal Eco Goals & Monthly Report Export 📄 (New)

Students can set custom daily CO₂ targets on Profile with an interactive progress ring, and export a downloadable monthly carbon report PNG card for sharing.

12. Daily Eco Digest & Global Comparison 🌍 (New)

Dashboard now features a daily rotating Mumbai eco-fact digest and horizontal comparison bars contrasting individual emissions with Mumbai, India, USA, and World averages.

What Makes This Different

  • Not just an app — it connects to physical campus: QR codes at actual locations make the habit verification real, not self-reported guesses.
  • Emotional engagement: Seeing your avatar wilt when you skip habits is far more powerful than seeing a number go up.
  • AI-powered daily motivation: The twin's message changes every day and is unique to the student's current state.
  • Collective impact visible: The campus forest makes the entire college's effort visible to everyone, creating community pride.
  • Real data, not fake: The leaderboard is powered by actual Firebase Firestore — every other student's score updates on your screen in real time.
  • Installable like an app: It's a PWA — students can install it on their phone home screen with no app store needed.
