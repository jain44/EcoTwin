Problem Statement
Why EcoTwin exists
😴

Student Disengagement

Generic sustainability apps fail students — abstract CO₂ numbers don't connect to daily campus life, leading to zero habit change.

📊

No Accountability Layer

Students have no peer comparison, no campus-wide view of collective impact, and no consequence/reward for individual actions.

🏫

No Faculty Visibility

Faculties and sustainability officers have no real-time dashboard to track department-level adoption or campus CO₂ trends.

🤖

No Personalization

One-size-fits-all apps don't adapt to individual commute, diet, and energy patterns, making guidance irrelevant.

✅ EcoTwin's Solution

EcoTwin transforms sustainability into a campus-wide game. Each student gets a living digital twin avatar that visually evolves based on their real carbon footprint data. A Tamagotchi-style character, powered by Groq's Llama-3 AI, gives daily personalized encouragement. Real-time Firebase leaderboards create healthy rivalry between departments, QR codes at campus locations verify in-person habit logging, and a shared Campus Forest visualizes collective impact. Faculty get a live analytics dashboard — zero manual reporting needed.

Feature Breakdown
9 core features, fully implemented
🌿 Digital Eco-Twin Avatar
Core Feature
  • SVG avatar with 3 visual states: Thriving 🌳 / Neutral 🌱 / Wilting 🥀
  • Framer Motion breathing, swaying & glow animations
  • State driven by 7-day rolling CO₂ average
  • AI Personality via Groq Llama-3 — unique daily message, Firestore-cached
📊 Carbon Calculator & Habit Log
Core Feature
  • 3 categories: Commute · Diet · Energy Usage
  • Mumbai student baseline: 5.5 kg CO₂/day
  • Photo proof upload (base64, stored locally)
  • QR-verified logging: +15 Trust Score bonus
🔮 Predictive Emission Warning
AI / ML Feature
  • Linear regression on last 7 days → 3-day forecast
  • Color-coded alerts: 🟢 On track / 🟡 Rising / 🔴 Warning
  • Specific recommended actions per alert level
  • Auto-displayed on Dashboard when trend is upward
⚔️ EcoBattles — Live Leaderboard
Real-time Firebase
  • Live Firestore cross-device leaderboard (not fake localStorage)
  • 15 seeded students across all 8 TCET departments
  • Filter by: All Students / Department / Hostel
  • Shows rank change, rolling avg, Green Coins, Trust Score
🌳 Campus Forest
Public Page
  • No-login public page at /campus-forest
  • Every student who logs habits plants a tree
  • Tree size & color = student's sustainability score
  • Real-time Firestore listener — forest grows live
📍 QR Code Location Proof
Physical Integration
  • 6 campus QR location cards (printable from /qr-locations)
  • Locations: Train Station, Canteen, Library, Eco Garden, Hostel, Sports
  • QR scan pre-fills habit form + marks as "QR Verified"
  • Camera modal via html5-qrcode library
🎓 Admin / Faculty Dashboard
Analytics
  • Password-gated (/admin)
  • KPI cards: Total Twins · CO₂ Prevented · Avg Footprint · App Adoption
  • Department bar chart (Chart.js) — color-coded green/amber/red
  • Real-time Firestore listener — live updates
🪙 GreenCoins Marketplace
Gamification
  • Earn coins per habit log — streak multiplier boosts earnings
  • Trust Score (0–100) adjusts coin rate
  • Redeem: Canteen 10% Off · Library Printing · Tree Planting · Bike Pass
🏅 Achievement Badges
Gamification
  • 8 badges: First Step · Week Warrior · Carbon Hero · Commute King
  • Diet Champ · Power Saver · QR Scout · Eco Legend
  • Unlocked dynamically from habit log analysis

What Makes EcoTwin Unique
Innovation pillars that differentiate from standard sustainability apps
🦎

Tamagotchi-Style Twin

A living avatar that visually wilts when you skip habits — emotional design creates far stronger habit loops than charts alone.

📍

Physical QR Integration

Campus QR codes at real locations bridge digital logging to physical spaces — verifying students actually went to the canteen, library, etc.

🤖

LLM AI Personality

Groq Llama-3 generates a unique plant-themed motivational message every day — cached in Firestore so zero repeat API calls.

🔮

Predictive Forecasting

Linear regression on 7-day data predicts your 3-day emissions trajectory — gives students actionable warnings before they overshoot.

🏆

Department Rivalry

Branch-vs-branch leaderboard leverages existing TCET college culture — turns sustainability into a competitive sport students care about.

🌳

Collective Campus Forest

A public shared garden that grows in real-time as students log — makes invisible collective impact beautifully visible to everyone.
